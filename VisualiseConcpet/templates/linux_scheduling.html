CPU Scheduling

How Linux manages multi-tasking and switches between processes.

1. Context Switching

When the OS switches from Process A to Process B, it must save the current state (Registers, PC) and load the new one.

PCB A
Registers: [saved]
CPU CORE
Running: Process A
PCB B
Registers: [idle]

2. Scheduling Algorithms

FCFS

First-Come, First-Served. Simple but can lead to the "Convoy Effect".

SJF

Shortest Job First. Minimizes average waiting time but needs prediction.

Round Robin

Time-sharing. Each process gets a "Time Quantum" before being swapped.

Priority

Highest priority runs first. Can lead to "Starvation" of low priority tasks.

The ReAct (Reason + Act) Loop

User Task: "Find the weather in Tokyo and multiply the temperature by 2."

Thought: I need to find the current weather in Tokyo first. I'll use the WebSearch tool.
Action: WebSearch("current temperature Tokyo")
Observation: The current temperature in Tokyo is 15°C.

Thought: Now I need to multiply 15 by 2. I'll use the Calculator tool.
Action: Calculator("15 * 2")
Observation: 30.

Thought: I have the final answer.
Final Answer: The temperature in Tokyo is 15°C, and multiplying it by 2 gives 30.

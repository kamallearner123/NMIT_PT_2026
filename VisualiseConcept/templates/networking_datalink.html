The Data Link Layer

Hop-to-Hop delivery using MAC addresses and ARP resolution.

1. The Ethernet Frame

Everything sent over a local wire is wrapped in an Ethernet frame. It uses MAC Addresses to find the destination device.

2. ARP: Mapping IP to MAC

Computers know IP addresses, but switches only know MAC addresses. **ARP** is the bridge between them.

ARP Request (Broadcast)

"WHO HAS 192.168.1.5? TELL 192.168.1.10"

➔

ARP Reply (Unicast)

"I AM 192.168.1.5. MY MAC IS 00:AA:BB:CC:DD:EE"

kamal@linux:~$ arp -a
? (192.168.1.1) at 50:c7:bf:32:4d:11 [ether] on eth0
? (192.168.1.5) at 00:aa:bb:cc:dd:ee [ether] on eth0
